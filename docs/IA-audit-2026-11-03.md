# Serenity v2 — IA audit (2026-11-03)

## Problem
Five tabs (Checklist, Present, Tools, Story, Analytics) mirrored *when features were built*, not *how the day is lived*.
- "Do it now" exercises were split across Present and Tools.
- Emergency Reset sat at the bottom of Tools — 2 taps + scroll during a panic.
- Two reframe features lived in two tabs.
- Checklist showed all 16 tasks at once regardless of time of day.

## New structure (4 tabs + SOS)
| Tab | Job | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | What do I do now? | Greeting + progress, quick actions (Check in, Breathe, Back to it, Be present), daily plan with the current phase open and tagged "Now" |
| **Practice** | Do a short exercise | Observe, Body focus, Back to it, Catch a judgment, Box breathing, Affirm & cope |
| **Reflect** | Slower written work | Quick reframe, Guided reframe, Effort ledger (nightly), Four pillars (weekly) |
| **Progress** | How am I doing? | 7-day chart (+ Present minutes), check-in journey |
| **SOS** (top bar, every screen) | Panic moment | Emergency resets, first one open, "Breathe now" |

Old URLs redirect (/checklist, /tools, /present, /story/*, /analytics). PWA shortcuts: Back to it, SOS.
